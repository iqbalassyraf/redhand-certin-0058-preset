Rationale

CIAD-2026-0058.R0.agent_quarantined

The agent run, or its token subject, is quarantined. After a containment-worthy detection every later call from the run (or subject) is refused, so an attacker cannot keep probing and cannot flood the audit log.

CIAD-2026-0058.R1.token_invalid

The agent token failed verification, or was revoked. Every decision keys on the agent run id, which is only trustworthy once a short-lived signed token has verified; an unauthenticated call never touches another run's state.

CIAD-2026-0058.R1.staging_to_prod_pivot

A credential minted for one environment was used against another. Staging-to-production pivoting is the first step of the CRAFTY FALCON chain; a token is bound to one environment and any other use quarantines the run.

CIAD-2026-0058.R2.micro_tx_probe

Micro-value payments repeated inside a short window. Attackers verify unauthorised fund flows with tiny payments; the first is indistinguishable from a legitimate charge, so the breaker trips on the second.

CIAD-2026-0058.R2.velocity_burst

Machine-speed bursts of calls, per tool and across all tools of a run. Enumeration and card-range scraping run far faster than a legitimate agent; counting across all tools stops an attacker spreading calls over many tools.

CIAD-2026-0058.R2.structuring

Several payments that together exceed a threshold no single payment crosses. Splitting a large payout into sub-threshold payments evades a per-payment gate; totals are added up per run, per token subject and per payee across runs, and across currencies when aggregation is configured.

CIAD-2026-0058.R2.recon_to_payment_sequence

Reconnaissance calls followed by a payment within a window. Automated discovery immediately followed by a payout is the shape of the kill chain, not of ordinary use.

CIAD-2026-0058.R2.cumulative_cap

A rolling cap on what one run, and one subject, may move. Slow draining stays under every short window; a rolling cap needs step-up (or blocks) when a payment would take the total over it. Refused payments are not counted.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.tool_not_pinned

The tool is not in the token's pinned allowed_tools. Least privilege: a run may call only the tools its token names, so scope expansion toward settlement or transfer tools is refused and contained.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.hitl_required

A high-value call on a protected tool needs human step-up. Settlement and disbursement above a per-currency threshold must not complete on an agent's say-so; the shield returns exactly what a human is asked to approve.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.new_beneficiary_high_value

A high-value payment to a payee that is not known. Diverting funds to a mule account is caught at a lower threshold than the general gate; every payee of a bulk call is checked.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.approval_accepted

A signed per-transaction approval was accepted. Records who approved which exact transaction, so a step-up is evidence, not a flag.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.approval_rejected

A presented approval was not honoured. An expired, forged, replayed or mismatched approval leaves step-up required and says why; it never falls back to the run-wide flag.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.legacy_run_wide_step_up

The deprecated run-wide hitl_step_up claim satisfied a step-up. The claim pre-authorises a whole run; each use is reported until it is switched off.

CIAD-2026-0058.R3.exemption_applied

A named, time-limited exemption waived a step-up requirement. Every waiver is attributable: the exemption id, approver, reason and expiry are logged.

CIAD-2026-0058.R4.off_hours_hitl_required

Protected tools need step-up in a low-monitoring window. Weekends, holidays and night hours are when passive supervision is weakest.

CIAD-2026-0058.R4.off_hours_tightened

Off-hours thresholds were applied to this call. Informational: limits are tightened in low-monitoring windows.

Tunable: a policy may run the rule in shadow, disable it or re-label it. Waivable: a time-limited exemption may waive a hitl_required violation of it.
