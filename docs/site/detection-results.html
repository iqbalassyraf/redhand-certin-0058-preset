Documented gaps

Attacks the preset does not catch, kept in the corpus so the limit is visible:

  • G-probe-first: The first probe is indistinguishable from a legitimate charge; the breaker trips on the second.
  • G-unbound: A tool that moves money through arguments the policy neither binds nor protects is not judged; bind it.
  • G-lowslow: Slow, small payments to a vetted payee stay under every window and the daily cap, by design.
  • G-stolen: A valid token is a valid identity; the shield cannot tell a thief from the agent before revocation.
  • G-justunder: A single payment below the threshold to a vetted payee is allowed; structuring needs two payments.

Documented false positives

Benign traffic the preset flags by design:

  • FP-coffee: Two micro-payments inside a minute trip the breaker even when legitimate, as the advisory specifies.
  • FP-refunds: Several small refunds inside a minute look like probing; spread them out.
  • FP-dashboard: More than 20 calls per second to one tool is treated as enumeration.
  • FP-weekend-settlement: Step-up is mandatory for protected tools off-hours, whatever the amount.
